How to Use the Simulator

Get running in under a minute. Advance a real catheter through the 3D-printed heart and watch the bedside monitor change as the tip floats through each chamber.

1

Power on the simulator by plugging it in

The Philips IntelliVue–style display comes up showing ECG and arterial pressure running continuously. Let it settle into a steady rhythm.

2

The simulator software should start automatically

If not, you can tap "" to start the software

3

Feed the catheter through the rollers and into the heart

The simulator knows how far the catheter has traveled through the heart by how many times the wheel has rotated

4

Reset between floats

Pull the catheter back to the start, then tap RESET TO SVC.

Patient Cases

Every patient except Perfect Patty uses real ICU patient waveforms.
1 Perfect Patty

Patty is not a real patient but an example of the textbook waveforms that you won't actually see very often.

2 Esther

Esther has some ectopy throughout the tracing and very elevated PA pressures. Also notice the respiratory variation in her wedge tracing

3 Herbert

Herbert has the cleanest CVP waveform with occasionally identifiable a, c, and v waves

4 Horace

Horace has the highest Pulmonary artery pressures of all the patients. He also demonstrates the importance of adjusting the scale on the monitor to see the whole waveform.

5 Grover

Grover's RV did not like the catheter and shows lots of ectopy when it's in there. He demonstrates pulmonary hypertension with PAP around 55/20
